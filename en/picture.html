PICTURE A SCIENTIST

Picture a Scientist (2020) is a powerful documentary directed by Ian Cheney and Sharon Shattuck that highlights the inequalities and discrimination suffered by women in the scientific community. Through the testimonies of researchers like Nancy Hopkins, Raychelle Burks and Jane Willenbring, the film reveals the invisible obstacles: sexism, harassment, lack of recognition, which they face.
